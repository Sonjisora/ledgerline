Refunds & Replacement Coverage

We stand behind every pre-verified account we deliver. Please review how pre-delivery cancellations, unused accounts, and our up to 30-day replacement warranty work:

1. Pre-Handover Cancellation (100% Refund)

If an ordered package is out of stock or has not yet been delivered within the agreed delivery window, the buyer may request a full 100% refund via the original payment method before credential handover.

2. Unused Account Return Window (Within 30 Days)

Unused accounts—where credentials have not been altered, no live transactions have been processed, no payout bank/IBAN has been linked, and no platform security flags have been triggered—may be reviewed for an exchange or partial/full credit within 30 days of purchase, subject to verification of the account's untouched state. Because digital account credentials and 2FA keys cannot be revoked once accessed and modified, cash or crypto refunds are not issued after credentials have been used, modified, or connected to a live store, except where replacement stock is unavailable under an active warranty claim.

3. Up to 30-Day Replacement Warranty

Eligible accounts include up to 30 days of replacement coverage from the date of delivery against initial verification holds or unexpected lockouts that occur despite following Ledgerline's handover guidelines. If a valid claim cannot be resolved with a matching replacement account within 48 hours, Ledgerline will issue a prorated or full refund depending on usage duration.

✓ COVERED BY WARRANTY
  • Dead-on-arrival login credentials or 2FA issues reported upon delivery
  • Pre-existing verification issues or initial KYC/KYB re-checks not caused by buyer store content
  • Unexpected lockouts while following the handover guidelines
✕ NOT COVERED
  • Prohibited or high-risk products, or customer chargebacks and disputes
  • Mismatched or flagged IPs/VPNs, shared proxies, or compromised devices
  • Changing core entity details immediately after login

FAQ

Questions founders ask first.

What is included with each account?

Full account access, original verified email, 2FA keys, KYC/KYB verification status, and a clean handover guide.

How does the 30-day warranty work?

If an account experiences an unexpected verification lockout or restriction within the warranty window (up to 30 days) while following our usage guidelines, we replace it.

Are the accounts warmed before delivery?

Yes. Accounts are aged and warmed with clean baseline activity so they don't trigger immediate new-account risk flags.

How fast is delivery?

In-stock US, UK, and EU accounts are delivered within a few hours of order confirmation.

Do I get full ownership and payout control?

Yes. Once handed over, you update the security credentials and route payouts directly to your own bank or IBAN.

Our Story

From an independent startup to a trusted community for verified account trading.

Ledgerline started as a lean e-commerce startup built to solve one recurring bottleneck: good stores getting stalled by cold-account holds and endless KYC/KYB delays. Led by James (@jamesaccounts), what began as a focused operation grew into an active community and marketplace (@buyshopifypayments) specializing in buying and selling pre-verified, warmed Stripe and Shopify accounts across 75+ countries.

Today, every handover is backed by public buyer #feedbacks, active #scammers alerts, full login and 2FA ownership transfer, and up to a 30-day replacement warranty.
